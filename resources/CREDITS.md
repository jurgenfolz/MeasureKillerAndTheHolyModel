# Credits

Third-party assets used in Measure Killer and The Holy Model.
Every asset below is free for commercial use.
CC0 assets need no credit, but we credit the authors anyway.

## Graphics

- `resources/Tiles` — by Anokolisa (https://www.patreon.com/Anokolisa), free for commercial use.
- `resources/enemies/`, `resources/items/`, `resources/effects/` and `resources/characters/pedro.png` are placeholder art drawn for this project.
  They use no third-party assets, so they can be replaced or kept freely.
- The hero in `resources/characters/measure_killer/` and the measure in `resources/enemies/measure/` were drawn by Klaus.
  Their animation strips were generated from those drawings with PixelLab (https://www.pixellab.ai).
- `resources/items/swords/29.png` is the hero's sword. Its author and license still need to be written here.

## Music (`resources/music/`)

All tracks are by nene and were converted from WAV to OGG Vorbis (quality 6), with no other change.
The sample count stays the same, so the tracks loop without a gap.

| File | Original name | Author | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `resources/music/menu.ogg` | "Boss Battle #2 [Symphonic Metal]", file `boss_battle_#2_metal_opening.wav` from `boss_battle_#2_metal_pack.zip` | nene | CC0 1.0 | https://opengameart.org/comment/99146 |
| `resources/music/level.ogg` | "Once More [Metal]", file `once_more_metal.wav` | nene | CC0 1.0 | https://opengameart.org/node/165075 |

The author notes that the two file names in the Boss Battle #2 ZIP are swapped.
`boss_battle_#2_metal_opening.wav` is the part that loops, so the menu uses that one.
